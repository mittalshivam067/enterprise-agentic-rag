NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK
All policies in this document are fictional and created for RAG testing.

Working hours
The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Employees should coordinate schedule changes with their manager.
A one-hour lunch break is recommended.

Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least five working days in advance when possible.
Emergency sick leave should be reported to the employee's manager as soon as practical.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Some roles may require additional office attendance based on business needs.
Employees working remotely must remain reachable during their agreed working hours.

Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,000.
The budget may be used for approved courses, technical certifications, and job-related conferences.
Manager approval is required before purchasing training.
